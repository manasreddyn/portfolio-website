About

I rarely leave a question alone.

One thing leads to another, a quick search turns into a rabbit hole, and before I know it, I'm exploring something completely unrelated to where I started. I want to know why things work the way they do, what assumptions they rely on, and what might happen if we approached them differently.

I'm fascinated by the space between understanding something and reimagining it. Taking an existing idea, looking at it from an unfamiliar angle, and finding a possibility that wasn't obvious before. That curiosity takes me across different fields, from electrical systems and control to software and AI.

Not every rabbit hole leads somewhere useful, and not every idea needs to become a project. Sometimes, understanding something is enough. Other times, a question refuses to leave me alone until I've tried building an answer.

I'm still figuring out where that curiosity will take me. That's probably the point.

Research Power Systems · Generative AI February 2026 – May 2026

Predictive and Adaptive Synthetic Inertia Control (PASIC) for Renewable Power Systems

A research-oriented control strategy designed to mitigate low-inertia vulnerabilities in renewable-dominated power grids through predictive machine learning and adaptive frequency stabilization.

Architected Five-Stage Control Pipeline
Sense → Predict → Compare → Decide → Actuate → Log
  • Developed a TimeGAN-based generative AI engine to synthesize realistic grid dynamic trajectories and forecast synthetic inertia control requirements.
  • Architected a five-stage closed-loop pipeline (Sense → Predict → Compare → Decide → Actuate → Log) for automated grid event response.
  • Integrated real-time frequency prediction with adaptive stabilization logic to mitigate Rate of Change of Frequency (RoCoF) excursions during contingency events.

Embedded Industrial IoT · Condition Monitoring March 2026 – April 2026

Smart Motor Health Monitoring and Predictive Maintenance System

An embedded sensor-fusion system built on ESP32-S3 that captures current, thermal dissipation, mechanical vibration, and rotational speed to provide real-time health classification and relay-isolated equipment protection.

  • Engineered an embedded monitoring system centered on the ESP32-S3 microcontroller for continuous telemetry acquisition and edge evaluation.
  • Implemented a three-tier motor health scoring algorithm classifying operating states into Normal, Warning, and Critical thresholds.
  • Unified multi-modal sensor fusion: ACS712 (current draw), LM35 (temperature), MPU6050 (tri-axial vibration), and Hall Effect Sensor (RPM measurement).
  • Integrated an I2C LCD for local real-time telemetry readout and automated relay-based protective cutoff to prevent motor burnout under hazardous conditions.

Research

Research Paper Submission

"Predictive and Adaptive Synthetic Inertia Control for Renewable-Dominated Power Systems Using Generative AI"

Author: Venkata Sai Manas Reddy Naguru · Reached Final Submission Stage at the IRAI Conference 2026

As power grids transition toward high penetrations of converter-interfaced renewable energy resources (solar photovoltaic and wind), conventional mechanical rotational inertia is significantly displaced. This research formulates a generative AI methodology utilizing TimeGAN to emulate adaptive synthetic inertia in real time.

  • Proposes synthetic inertia emulation to mitigate post-contingency frequency nadir and severe Rate of Change of Frequency (RoCoF).
  • Integrates time-series generative modeling to anticipate frequency disturbances prior to standard threshold breaches.
  • Formulates a closed-loop adaptive control scheme validated against dynamic power grid contingency scenarios.
